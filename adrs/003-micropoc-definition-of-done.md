# ADR-003: Micro-PoC definition of done (≤3 weeks)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Context:** “PoC” often means theater. Buyers need a bounded production wedge they can defend.

## Decision

A Micro-PoC is **done** only when all of the following exist:

1. **One decision** the CEO/operator can make after the readout
2. **One success metric** agreed on day 1 (numeric or binary gate)
3. **Architecture-first constraints** written (identity, data, seams, non-goals)
4. **Evidence pack:** what was measured, what failed, what was out of scope
5. **CEO-readable readout** (1–2 pages) + technical appendix
6. **Explicit next path:** stop / fix / expand / retainer (no vague “phase 2”)
7. **Calendar ≤3 weeks** from kickoff to readout (or the PoC is rescoped)

## Out of scope for a Micro-PoC

- Org-wide CoE setup
- Full platform replacement
- Pure success-fee delivery without a baseline fee
- Client secrets in public repos
